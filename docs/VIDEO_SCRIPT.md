## What must not be said

- No accelerator speed-up for our model: XGBoost, pandas and NetworkX run on the CPU. Say only what the counters show (`docs/ML_RESULTS.md` section 8).
- Not "incremental" and not "instant" or "real time": a full recompute in micro-batches, about ten to twelve seconds on the idle machine.
- The pig-butchering rings are simulated by our own team. The 0.45 and 0.15 are for IBM-only data and one seed; the 84 percent is for the demo bundle on simulated rings from the same generator as its training rings (`docs/ML_RESULTS.md` section 7, "Same generator for training and testing").
- Do not call an account a criminal (say flagged), and do not say warnings were sent.

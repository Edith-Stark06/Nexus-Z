## What must not be said

- No accelerator speed-up for our model: XGBoost, pandas and NetworkX run on the CPU. Say only what the counters show (`docs/ML_RESULTS.md` section 8).
- Not "incremental" and not "instant" or "real time": a full recompute in micro-batches, about ten seconds on the idle machine and up to about thirty-six while payments stream in.
- The pig-butchering rings are simulated by our own team. The 0.43 and 0.15 are for IBM-only data; the 80 and 63 percent are for the demo bundle on simulated rings from the same generator as its training rings, counting only rings with at least two active fraud accounts in the window (`docs/ML_RESULTS.md` section 7).
- Do not call an account a criminal (say flagged), and do not say warnings were sent (we list accounts that recently paid in; the rule is not evaluated).
- The Assistant, if shown: IBM Granite Embedding on the VM picks the topic; with the hosted answer writer on, the text comes from NVIDIA's model at NVIDIA, not from IBM and not from the VM. Say which badge is on the screen.
